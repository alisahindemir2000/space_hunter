This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Space Hunter (the "App"), how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Space Hunter does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described in Section 4.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so the game can remember your progress:

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display interstitial (full-screen) ads between levels. When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and, where you have given consent or where permitted by law, show personalized ads. We do not receive any information from Google that personally identifies you.

For more information on how Google uses data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the App displays a consent form provided by Google (Google User Messaging Platform) when you first open it. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalized ads. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

To change your choice later, clear the App's data (Settings → Apps → Space Hunter → Storage → Clear data) or reinstall the App; the consent form will be shown again the next time you open it.

Your advertising choices

On Android you can control ad personalization at any time:

  • Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device) to delete or reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Manage the ads Google shows you at https://myadcenter.google.com.

5. Children's privacy

Space Hunter is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App requests ads with Google's general-audience content rating so that ads are suitable for all ages.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google, for advertising purposes, as described in Section 4.

7. Data retention and security

Game progress and settings stay on your device until you delete them. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. We use the official Google Mobile Ads SDK, and all ad traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
